# Getting Started: Sign Up & Your Data

This guide walks you through creating your OptiNiSt account, explains what personal data we collect, and describes what happens when you download your data or delete your account.

---

## Creating Your Account

### What You'll Need

To sign up, you'll provide three pieces of information:

- **Name** — must be at least 2 characters
- **Email address** — a valid email you can access (you'll need to verify it)
- **Password** — see requirements below

### Password Requirements

Your password must be **between 6 and 255 characters** and contain at least:

- One **letter** (uppercase or lowercase)
- One **number**
- One **special character**

The allowed special characters are:

```
! # $ % & ( ) * + , - . / @ _ |
```

> **Tip:** Characters not in the list above (such as spaces, quotes, or brackets) are not accepted.

### Step-by-Step Sign-Up

1. Go to the **Sign Up** page and fill in the registration form: **Name**, **Email**, **Password**, and **Confirm Password**.
2. Optionally check **Show Password** to see what you're typing.
3. Click **Sign Up**.
4. If everything is valid, you'll see a confirmation screen titled **"Registration Almost Complete!"** with a message that a verification email has been sent to your address.
5. Check your inbox and click the verification link to finish creating your account. Once verified, you can log in.

---

## Verifying Your Email

### How Verification Works

After you submit the registration form, we send a verification email to the address you provided. Click the link in that email to activate your account. You won't be able to log in until your email is verified.

### Resending the Verification Email

If you don't see the email, click the **Resend Verification Email** button on the confirmation screen. After sending, the button is temporarily disabled for **60 seconds** to prevent duplicate emails.

### Troubleshooting

If you're having trouble receiving the verification email:

- **Check your spam or junk folder** — the email may have been filtered.
- **Verify the email address** — make sure you entered it correctly on the registration form.
- **Wait 60 seconds** before clicking **Resend Verification Email** again.
- **Contact support** if the problem persists — please [Contact Support](../other/contact.md).

## What Happens After Sign-Up

### Your Default Account Settings

When your account is created, it starts with these defaults:

| Setting | Default Value |
|---------|---------------|
| Plan | Free |
| Storage limit | 5 GB |
| Role | Operator |
| Cloud storage | Private bucket created for you |

### What Data We Store

Here is a summary of the personal and account data we keep on file:

| Data | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Name** | The name you entered during registration |
| **Email** | Your email address, used for login and notifications |
| **Password** | Managed by our authentication provider — never stored in plaintext by us |
| **Workspaces** | Your workflow projects and their contents |
| **Storage usage** | How much of your storage quota is in use |
| **Subscription info** | Your current plan (Free or Premium) and billing status |

---

## Downloading Your Data

You can download your data on a per-workspace basis. The following are available for download:

- **Workflow YAML files** — your workflow definitions
- **Input data** — the original files you uploaded
- **Output data** — result files in NWB format

> **Note:** Intermediate outputs (data generated between workflow steps) are not available for download because they can be regenerated by re-running the workflow.

If your premium subscription has ended, you have a **30-day grace period** to download your data before auto-deletion begins.

> For full details on what happens after a subscription ends, see the [Data Retention & Deletion Policy](../other/plan_expiration.md).

## Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings. Deletion is **permanent** and cannot be undone.

### What Gets Removed

When you delete your account, the following are permanently removed:

1. **Login credentials** — your authentication account is deleted and cannot be recovered.
2. **Subscription** — any active subscription is cancelled immediately.
3. **Cloud storage** — your private storage bucket and all its contents are permanently deleted.
4. **Workspaces** — all of your workspaces are removed.

### What Remains

After deletion, an **inactive record** of your name and email is retained in our database. This record is kept solely for:

- **Audit and compliance** — maintaining a log of past accounts
- **Abuse prevention** — preventing repeated misuse of the platform

This record cannot be used to log in or access any data. No workspace data, storage contents, or subscription details are retained.

### Re-Registering

Login credentials are fully removed during deletion. Your email address becomes available for sign up for a new account using the same email address. The new account will start fresh with default settings — no data or settings from the previous account will carry over.
