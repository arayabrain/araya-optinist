Pricing
=======

Araya-OptiNiSt Cloud offers three plans to suit researchers at every scale.

### Free Plan

Get started at no cost with access to the full OptiNiSt analysis pipeline.

- **Storage**: 5 GB
- **Compute**: Shared cloud resources (auto-scaled based on demand)
- **Features**: All core workflow, visualization, and recording features
- **Public Page**: Publish experiments for anyone to view and reproduce

### Premium Plan -- $20 / month

For researchers who need more storage and faster, dedicated compute.

- **Storage**: 200 GB
- **Compute**: Dedicated cloud resources (private instance assigned on login)
- **Features**: Everything in Free, plus priority compute and expanded storage
- **Billing**: Monthly via Stripe. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

If you cancel your Premium subscription:

- A 30-day grace period begins during which you retain full Premium access.
- If your stored data exceeds the 5 GB Free limit, you will be unable to run new analysis until you reduce your usage or re-subscribe.

### Custom Plans

For labs or institutions with specialized requirements, we offer custom plans with:

- **Compute**: Any size of CPU or GPU instance to match your workload.
- **Storage**: Any amount of cloud storage needed.
- **Custom Analysis**: Additional analysis methods and visualization plots can be developed on request.

Contact us at optinist-support@araya.org to discuss a custom plan.

### Getting Started

1. **Sign up** for a free account at the Araya-OptiNiSt Cloud portal.
2. **Upgrade** to Premium at any time from the Subscription page in the app.
3. **Contact us** at optinist-support@araya.org for custom plan inquiries or any questions.
